Start, stop and switch your Toggl Track timers from your wrist.
Toggl Timer puts your Toggl Track time tracking on the Pebble. See the running entry with project, client, elapsed time and today's total, stop it with one press, or start the next one from your favourites, your recent entries or the project list. A timer stopped on the phone shows up on the watch within half a minute.

Features
• Status screen with project colour, description, running time and daily total
• Favourite tiles: up to four entries you use every day, one tap to start
• Recent entries and all projects, with the time booked today
• Voice dictation of a new description, then pick the project (Pebble Time 2)
• Touch: tap to open the picker, swipe left for the previous entry, swipe right to stop (Pebble Time 2)
• Reminders: the watch wakes the app when a timer runs longer than you want or late in the evening, and optionally on weekday mornings when nothing is running
• Optional rounding to 5, 15 or 30 minutes when stopping
• Launcher glance with the running entry and its duration
• Languages: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish (follows the watch)

Buttons
UP refresh · SELECT start/stop, hold to dictate · DOWN favourites or list

Setup
Open the settings in the Pebble app and paste your Toggl API token (found at the bottom of track.toggl.com/profile). Type "demo" as the token to try the app with sample data.

Works with every Pebble; dictation and touch need the Pebble Time 2. Not affiliated with Toggl.
