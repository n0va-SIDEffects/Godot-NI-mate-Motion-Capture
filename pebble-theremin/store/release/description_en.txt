Play a theremin on your wrist: raise your hand for pitch, twist it for volume.

Theremin turns the Pebble Time 2 into an instrument you play without
touching it. Just like the original, the position of your hand shapes the
sound: raising and lowering your hand changes the pitch, twisting your wrist
controls the volume. The tone is synthesized in real time and played through
the watch's speaker.

Features

- Four waveforms: sine, triangle, square, sawtooth
- Up to four octaves of range, selectable lowest note
- Snap to scales: chromatic, major, minor, pentatonic, or play continuously like a real theremin
- Freely assignable control axes: lift, roll or compass, each invertible
- Adjustable sensitivity, portamento and volume
- Automatic calibration on launch: hold the watch still for a second and you're set
- Live readout of frequency and note name, with the animated waveform in the background
- Noise gate switches the speaker off during silence
- Every setting available on the watch or conveniently in the Pebble phone app

Controls

- SELECT: sound on or off
- UP and DOWN: waveform
- Long SELECT: settings
- Long UP: recalibrate the neutral position

Tip: Hold your arm relaxed in front of you and keep the watch still for a
moment when the app starts. That pose becomes the middle of the pitch range.
Tilted slightly towards you is loud, tilted away goes silent.

Note: The app needs a speaker and runs on the Pebble Time 2 only. Compass
mode is experimental.
